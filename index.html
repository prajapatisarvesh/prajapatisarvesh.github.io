I am a first-year PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Northeastern University. My research interests span robot perception, machine learning, control, and optimization. Currently, my research focuses on developing autonomous wheelchairs that empower individuals to move independently.

I'm also interested in human-robot interaction and robotic manipulation, and in bridging the gap between theoretical advances in robotics and their deployment on meaningful, real-world problems.
